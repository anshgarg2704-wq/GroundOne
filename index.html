About GroundOne

We Make the Ground Reality Visible.

As businesses grow, maintaining a consistent customer experience across every outlet becomes increasingly difficult.

GroundOne helps businesses see what is really happening on the ground — beyond reports, assumptions, and occasional visits.

Through independent mystery shopping, outlet audits, and customer experience evaluations, we capture real-world observations and turn them into clear, actionable intelligence.

Our goal is simple: help businesses see better, know more, and grow smarter.

Our Services

See Your Business Through a Different Lens.

We turn real-world observations into clear intelligence that helps businesses improve customer experience, strengthen operations, and maintain consistency across every outlet.

EXPERIENCE 01

Mystery Shopping

Experience your business through the eyes of a real customer. Our mystery shoppers visit your outlets as genuine customers and evaluate the complete experience — from arrival and staff interaction to service, product presentation, upselling, billing, and farewell.

What we evaluate:

  • Customer service & staff behaviour
  • Product & service experience
  • Upselling & product knowledge
  • Outlet ambience
  • Billing & checkout
  • Overall customer experience

Outcome: A clear, evidence-backed view of what your customers actually experience.

EXECUTION 02

Outlet Audits

Know whether every outlet is meeting your standards. We evaluate your outlets against defined operational, brand, hygiene, and service standards to identify inconsistencies and gaps.

What we evaluate:

  • Brand & visual standards
  • Staff grooming & uniform
  • Hygiene & cleanliness
  • Operational procedures & SOPs
  • Product display & presentation

Outcome: A structured view of where your outlets are performing — and where they need attention.

INSIGHT 03

Customer Experience

Understand the moments that shape customer perception. We assess the customer journey to uncover service gaps, friction points, inconsistencies, and opportunities to create a better experience.

We look at:

  • Customer journey & touchpoints
  • Service quality & consistency
  • Friction points & customer pain points
  • Satisfaction & improvement areas

Outcome: Actionable insights that help turn better experiences into stronger customer relationships.
